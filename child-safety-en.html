Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: February 15, 2024
Last Updated: January 14, 2026

This Child Safety Standards policy applies to all applications and services operated by SMK (hereinafter referred to as “the Company”).

The Company is committed to protecting minors and maintaining a safe digital environment across all our products. These standards define our zero-tolerance approach toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and describe the measures we take to ensure child safety.

1. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

The Company strictly prohibits any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including but not limited to:

  • The creation, distribution, possession, solicitation, or promotion of child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Grooming, sexual communication, or any inappropriate interaction involving minors
  • Any content or behavior that exploits, endangers, or harms minors

Any violation of this policy will result in immediate account suspension or permanent termination. Such violations may also be reported to relevant law enforcement or child protection authorities in accordance with applicable laws.

2. Age Restriction

  • Our applications are intended exclusively for users who are 18 years of age or older (or the applicable age of majority in your region).
  • The Company does not knowingly allow minors to use the Service.
  • Accounts suspected of belonging to minors may be suspended or permanently removed without prior notice.

3. In-App Reporting and Safety Measures

Our applications provide built-in mechanisms that allow users to maintain a safe environment within the Service.

  • Users can report inappropriate content or user behavior, including suspected CSAE or CSAM, through the in-app inquiry and reporting features available in each application.
  • Users may also block other users directly within the app.

CSAE/CSAM Handling:

When reports of CSAE or CSAM are received through the in-app reporting mechanism, the Company will promptly:

  • Remove the content from the Service
  • Suspend or terminate accounts involved
  • Take appropriate actions, including reporting to law enforcement and relevant child protection authorities, such as the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or regional authorities, as required by law

All reports are reviewed promptly in accordance with internal policies and applicable legal requirements.

4. Legal Compliance

The Company complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including mandatory reporting obligations related to child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Where legally required, the Company cooperates fully with law enforcement agencies and relevant authorities.

6. External Reporting Resources (Optional)

Users may also report suspected child sexual abuse material to appropriate authorities, such as the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC), through their official reporting platforms.

7. Enforcement Actions

Violations of these Child Safety Standards may result in:

  • Immediate removal of content
  • Temporary or permanent account suspension
  • Reporting to law enforcement authorities where required by law

8. Policy Updates

The Company may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time to reflect changes in laws, regulations, or operational practices.

Any updates will be published on the official policy page.
