Frequently asked questions

About TikiLingo

What is TikiLingo?

TikiLingo is an Android language exchange app with easy random matching, simultaneous live voice and automatically translated text chat, 1:1 and group voice study rooms, partner search filters, AI message suggestions, anonymous chat, block and report controls, and back-and-forth text messaging that continues when a partner is offline.

How can I find a language exchange partner?

Use quick random matching or search for partners by age, gender, country, and language.

Can I use voice and text chat at the same time?

Yes. TikiLingo lets people chat live using voice and text at the same time.

Does TikiLingo automatically translate text chats?

Yes. Text chat messages are automatically translated to support conversations across languages.

What kinds of voice study rooms are available?

TikiLingo offers 1:1 private voice study rooms and public group voice study rooms.

Can AI help me start a conversation?

Yes. AI message recommendations suggest icebreakers and topics to help keep voice and text conversations going.

Can I chat anonymously and manage unwanted conversations?

TikiLingo supports anonymous chat without requiring a public profile, and includes block and report tools for managing conversations.

Can I keep messaging when someone is offline?

Yes. You can keep the conversation going by exchanging text messages back and forth even when the other person is offline, then return to it later.
