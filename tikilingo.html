Frequently asked questions

About TikiLingo

What is TikiLingo?

TikiLingo is an Android app for meeting people worldwide through anonymous text chat, random matching, and ongoing messaging. You can also use conversations to practice languages.

Can I use TikiLingo for language exchange?

Yes. The app supports conversations with people who speak the language you want to practice. The current Google Play listing describes language practice through real conversations and messaging.

Does TikiLingo offer live voice chat?

The current Google Play listing describes anonymous text chat and messaging, and does not list live voice chat. Check the listing for the current app features.

Does TikiLingo automatically translate chats?

Automatic chat translation is not listed as a TikiLingo feature on Google Play. The listing describes language practice through conversations and AI message suggestions.

Where can I download TikiLingo?

Use the official Google Play page.
